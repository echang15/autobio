A Boring Immigrant's Lens

Eric — born March 1982, New Jersey. Raised in Delaware.

I'm a software engineer with a couple decades and a handful of patents behind me, a hockey player who never quite retired, and a guitarist who peaked in a college dorm room. I'm also the son of Taiwanese immigrants who somehow ended up raising a kid in Delaware — the only one of us who isn't back in Taiwan. On paper, none of this is unusual: one college, a couple of employers over twenty years, a marriage that took embarrassingly long to happen. But an ordinary life still runs on real stakes — the weight of being the outsider's kid, the accident of geography that put a cousin two blocks from a burning building, the years that never show up on a resume. This is that life, written down as I remember it, one chapter at a time.

This is a work in progress. Chapters are added as they're written.
