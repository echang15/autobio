About This Project

Why this exists, and how it's being written.

This is my autobiography — a record of a fairly ordinary life, seen through the lens of growing up the child of immigrants in the United States. Nothing here is extraordinary. That's rather the point: most immigrant stories are not sagas, just quiet, cumulative years — and I wanted to write mine down before I forget the details.

It's written in my own words, published as I go, and organized roughly by chapter of life rather than strict chronology.

Mechanically: everything here is brain-dumped by me, in my own words, and assembled into these pages by Claude Code.

Built with care, one chapter at a time.
